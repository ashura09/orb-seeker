Orb Seeker

Seven numbered orbs are scattered far across the valley, always in rainbow order: 1 is red, 7 is violet. Your finder senses them, but only shows which is which once you're close. Gather all seven and the Keeper, a dragon of the valley, grants a wish. Gather them in order, 1 to 7, and it grants three.

Wanderers will challenge you to tap duels. Win fragments, spend them at the trader's cart, and everything you buy or wish for lands in the world for you to pick up and keep.
